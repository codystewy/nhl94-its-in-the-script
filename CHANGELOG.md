# Changelog

Every release of **NHL94 – It's In The Script**, newest first, in plain English.
Version numbers follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org): **MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH**. MAJOR goes up for big shake-ups, MINOR for new stuff, PATCH for fixes and polish.

## [2.2.0] – 2026-10-07

### ✨ New
- **League Discord, one click away.** A **+ League Discord** button now sits next to the League and Level pickers. Paste your league's server invite or channel link once, the button turns blue, and from then on it's a quick pass straight to the league chat. Every league and level keeps its own link.
- **Manage them in ⚙ Settings.** Change or remove any league's link, or clear them all.

## [2.1.0] – 2026-10-05

### ✨ New
- **The whole stats room gets new gear.** Standings, team rosters, player stat leaders, league records, all-time user standings, site records and box scores now all wear the same night-rink look as the coach page, Day mode included.
- **Standings with faces.** Every team shows its logo and coach, PTS stands out, streaks glow green or red, and your own team gets a ★ My Team box with its spot in the division.
- **Rosters in team colours.** Each roster page dresses in that team's jersey, with the coach, a Discord DM button, a team picker and a stat strip up top.
- **Box scores, broadcast style.** Big logos, a pixel scoreboard, the period-by-period line and side-by-side bars for shots, faceoffs, checks and more. The winner's colours take over the page.
- **Records you can read at a glance.** Every leader board gets its own card, and the leader's number shines in gold.
- **Sort and flip pages like before.** Every dropdown, sort and page button works just as it did, and the column you sorted by lights up.

## [2.0.1] – 2026-10-05

### 🐛 Fixed
- The new-look switch still wore its old **V1** jersey after the big 2.0 trade. It now shows **v2**, and it'll update itself at the next big release.

## [2.0.0] – 2026-10-05

### ✨ New
- **💬 DM any coach on Discord.** Press **+ DM** next to a coach, paste a message link from your DM with them, and the button turns into **CHAT**. One click and you're asking "game tonight?" A quick walkthrough shows you how the first time. Your links are yours alone, so they won't open anything for someone you share them with.
- **⚙ Settings, RetroArch style.** The gear in the corner opens a menu that looks just like your emulator's: arrow keys, Enter, Esc. See everything the script remembers, clear one thing at a time, flip Day and Night, replay the intros, and back up or restore it all. It all stays in your own browser.
- **Filter by Coach.** The row of coach pills is now one big dropdown with each coach's logo, name and DM button. Pick one coach or a whole line.
- **League bar up top.** Season and level pickers sit right under the menu, where the original site trained you to look. Type to search all ~286 seasons, and your own leagues wear a ★.
- **One switch for the new look.** The corner is now just an on/off switch (new look or the original site) and the gear.
- **Pick your channel.** Install Stable for tested releases, Latest for every change the moment it lands, or Fun for the playful extras. Settings shows which one you're on.
- **My Leagues speaks up.** Brighter buttons, and a one-time slap shot shows newcomers where their teams live. The **?** badge replays it.

### 🐛 Fixed
- Coach names and gold touches were squinting at you in Day mode (Calgary's gold was the worst offender). They're easy to read now.

## [1.4.1] – 2026-10-04

### ✨ New
- **Day & Night mode on every page.** Hit ☀ or ☾ in the bottom-right corner. Night is the default.
- **Every team page wears its own jersey.** All 26 teams get their 1993-94 colors, from Jets blue to Kings black and silver. The home page keeps the original site's red, white and blue.
- **★ My Leagues.** Playing in several leagues? Add each of your teams once and jump between them from any page. Back up your list to a file, restore it on another computer, or wipe it clean. No login needed.

### 🎨 Looks
- The home page wears the original site's colors again, so it feels like home.
- Removed the "pin your team" nag box from the home page.

### 🐛 Fixed
- The New York Rangers ran a line change and became unclickable in the standings (the site calls them "New York" there). They're back on the ice.

## [1.1.2] – 2026-10-04

### 🎨 Looks
- On smaller windows the menu folds into a tidy **☰ Menu** button instead of the logo body-checking the navigation.

### 📖 Docs
- Added a step-by-step install guide for Chrome, Brave, Firefox and Edge.

## [1.1.1] – 2026-10-04

### 🐛 Fixed
- Pages no longer spill off the side of your phone.

### 📖 Docs
- Added before & after screenshots.

## [1.1.0] – 2026-10-04

### ✨ New
- **The home page gets the makeover too**, with latest scores that show logos, coaches and who won.
- The script now runs across the whole site. Pages that aren't redesigned yet stay exactly as they were.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-04

### ✨ New
- **The coach page, redesigned.** Coach names next to every team, the schedule grouped by opponent, filters that remember you, and a checkpoint countdown.
- A **Classic / V1** switch so the original site is always one click away.
