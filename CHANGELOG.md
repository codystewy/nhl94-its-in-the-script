# Changelog

Every release of **NHL94 – It's In The Script**, newest first, in plain English.
Version numbers follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org): **1.MINOR.PATCH**. MINOR goes up for new stuff, PATCH for fixes and polish.

## [1.4.1] – 2026-10-04

### ✨ New
- **Day & Night mode on every page.** Hit ☀ or ☾ in the bottom-right corner. Night is the default.
- **Every team page wears its own jersey.** All 26 teams get their 1993-94 colors, from Jets blue to Kings black and silver. The home page keeps the original site's red, white and blue.
- **★ My Leagues.** Playing in several leagues? Add each of your teams once and jump between them from any page. Back up your list to a file, restore it on another computer, or wipe it clean. No login needed.

### 🎨 Looks
- The home page wears the original site's colors again, so it feels like home.
- Removed the "pin your team" nag box from the home page.

### 🐛 Fixed
- The New York Rangers ran a line change and became unclickable in the standings (the site calls them "New York" there). They're back on the ice.

## [1.1.2] – 2026-10-04

### 🎨 Looks
- On smaller windows the menu folds into a tidy **☰ Menu** button instead of the logo body-checking the navigation.

### 📖 Docs
- Added a step-by-step install guide for Chrome, Brave, Firefox and Edge.

## [1.1.1] – 2026-10-04

### 🐛 Fixed
- Pages no longer spill off the side of your phone.

### 📖 Docs
- Added before & after screenshots.

## [1.1.0] – 2026-10-04

### ✨ New
- **The home page gets the makeover too**, with latest scores that show logos, coaches and who won.
- The script now runs across the whole site. Pages that aren't redesigned yet stay exactly as they were.

## [1.0.0] – 2026-10-04

### ✨ New
- **The coach page, redesigned.** Coach names next to every team, the schedule grouped by opponent, filters that remember you, and a checkpoint countdown.
- A **Classic / V1** switch so the original site is always one click away.
